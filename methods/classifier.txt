RNA realism classifier

We trained a protein-conditioned binary classifier to distinguish real RBP-bound RNAs from composition-matched negatives. Positives were motif-balanced ProRiboGen pairs; each negative was obtained by nucleotide shuffling of the same RNA (length and composition preserved) under the same protein identity.

The model initializes from the pretrained ProRiboGen generator (6 layers) with ESM2-3B protein embeddings (d = 2560). After the encoder, RNA tokens attend to protein tokens (8-head cross-attention); CLS / mean / max / attention pooling and a bilinear RNA-protein score are concatenated and mapped by an MLP to a logit z. The predicted probability is

    p = sigma(z).

With the ESM trunk frozen except AdaLN and protein cross-attention, we optimize

    L = L_BCE + 0.5 * L_rank,

    L_rank = E[ max(0, m - (z+ - z-)) ],

where m = 0.5 and (z+, z-) are scores of a positive-negative pair for the same protein. Training used AdamW (head 5e-4, conditioning 1e-5), cosine decay, paired batches, two-epoch head warmup, then joint updates; the best validation checkpoint (epoch 2) was retained. On the held-out test set the model reached AUROC 0.878 (accuracy 0.788 at threshold 0.5).
